## Why This Works Financially

1. **Waste-derived feedstock** → low and stable input costs (rice husk at ₹2,000/ton vs synthetic silica at ₹15,000–20,000/ton)
2. **Regenerable zeolite** → recurring CO₂ capture value without replacement every cycle
3. **Multiple revenue streams** → carbon credits + adsorbent sales + CO₂ utilization reduces single-stream risk
4. **Modular scale-up** → capital efficiency; start small, expand based on demand
5. **Policy tailwinds** → CCUS incentives, Net Zero 2070, ESG compliance requirements all support adoption
